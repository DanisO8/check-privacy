Check: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

In short: Your vehicles, mileage, maintenance and trips stay on your phone. The app does not upload them, and it has no accounts. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which can collect some device information to show them.

Who we are

Check (“the app”) is a vehicle maintenance and mileage tracker published by anis. If you have any question about this policy, write to anis.mou012@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

The information you enter is saved only in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • Your vehicles (name, type, year, and unit of distance).
  • Mileage readings and maintenance items, with their dates, costs and notes.
  • The distance of trips detected by the optional trip tracking.
  • Your settings, such as reminder times and theme.

The app does not send this information to us or to anyone else, and it is never shared with advertisers. The app works without an internet connection. Only the ads need one.

Advertising

The app is free and is supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, the AdMob service, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app, may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • Your device’s advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived.
  • How you interact with ads, and diagnostic and performance information.

This is used for showing ads (including personalized ads where you allow them), measuring ads, preventing fraud, and improving the service. We do not receive your advertising ID or this information ourselves. Google’s own privacy policy explains how Google uses it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy options, when that option is shown.
  • On your Android phone you can turn off personalized ads or reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).

Location

The app asks for your location only if you turn on Detect trips with GPS in Settings. It is used to measure the approximate distance of your trips, and it works like this:

  • The location is processed on your phone while tracking is on, and Android shows a notification while it runs.
  • Only the distance of each trip is saved. Your route, your coordinates and the places you visit are not stored by the app and are not given to advertisers.
  • You can turn tracking off at any time in the app, or remove the location permission in your phone’s settings.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are created and shown by the app on your phone. They do not involve any server.

Backups and sharing

The “Export backup” feature creates a file with your data. The app then opens your phone’s share menu, and you decide where the file goes (for example Google Drive, an email to yourself, or your Files app). The app itself does not upload it anywhere.

Android may also include the app’s data in your device backup if you have turned on backup in your phone’s settings. That is handled by Android and your Google account, not by this app.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell your vehicle, mileage, maintenance or trip data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app stores with Settings → Reset application data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone. Backup files you exported yourself stay wherever you saved them, and you can delete them there. Information that Google collects for advertising is controlled through your Google account and your phone’s ad settings, described above.

Children

The app is for general use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the ads are not shown as child-directed.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the effective date above will change.
