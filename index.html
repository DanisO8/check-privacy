Check: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

In short: Check keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not send your data anywhere.

Who we are

Check (“the app”) is a vehicle maintenance and mileage tracker published by anis. If you have any question about this policy, write to anis.mou012@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The information you enter is saved only in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • Your vehicles (name, type, year, and unit of distance).
  • Mileage readings and maintenance items, with their dates, costs and notes.
  • The distance of trips detected by the optional trip tracking.
  • Your settings, such as reminder times and theme.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not need an internet connection and does not use one to transfer your data.

Location

The app asks for your location only if you turn on Detect trips with GPS in Settings. It is used to measure the approximate distance of your trips, and it works like this:

  • The location is processed on your phone while tracking is on, and Android shows a notification while it runs.
  • Only the distance of each trip is saved. Your route, your coordinates and the places you visit are not stored and are not transmitted.
  • You can turn tracking off at any time in the app, or remove the location permission in your phone’s settings.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are created and shown by the app on your phone. They do not involve any server.

Backups and sharing

The “Export backup” feature creates a file with your data. The app then opens your phone’s share menu, and you decide where the file goes (for example Google Drive, an email to yourself, or your Files app). The app itself does not upload it anywhere.

Android may also include the app’s data in your device backup if you have turned on backup in your phone’s settings. That is handled by Android and your Google account, not by this app.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything with Settings → Reset application data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone. Backup files you exported yourself stay wherever you saved them, and you can delete them there.

Children

The app is for general use and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example by adding online features), this page will be updated before the change is released, and the effective date above will change.
